# Contributing

Use the contribution action beside the explanation, concept or claim you are reading, or open `/contribute`.

You can improve an explanation, attach a supporting or conflicting study finding, correct a definition or identifier mapping, or add a research question. Evidence contributions identify a source, experimental comparison, population or strains, sample, measured outcome and material reviewed. A DOI, PMID or supported ENA accession resolves to a source record. If several reports describe one study, select the existing study record.

A reviewer checks the source and scope before acceptance changes the shared knowledge. Public authorship credit is optional; contact details stay private. The same accepted change appears in the connected topic and evidence views. Suggestions made against changed records require a fresh review.

Text selection and reading-note suggestions also work in the anatomy, food, mechanism, resource and paper interfaces. Contributors can propose specific wording and identify missing evidence directly.
